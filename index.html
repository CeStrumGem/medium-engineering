Engineering the medium

We engineer the state of complex media.

Medium Engineering is an engineering discipline for controlling the physical state of complex media — so that what is difficult to separate, recover or treat can become accessible.

Discover the approach Start a conversation
01 / The discipline

Change the medium, not only the process.

Industrial media are rarely simple mixtures. Their behavior can be governed by dispersion, aggregation, interfaces, surface effects and structural states.

Conventional treatment often changes chemistry while leaving much of that structure untouched. Medium Engineering starts from a different question: can the state of the medium itself be engineered?

01

Complex medium

Water, minerals, hydrocarbons, residues and dispersed systems.

02

Structural state

Understand how components interact, disperse and remain inaccessible.

03

Controlled action

Apply a defined physical impulse to alter the system state.

04

Accessibility

Enable separation, recovery or treatment that was previously difficult.

The central idea

We do not simply treat waste as waste.

We engineer the medium until its components become accessible — turning a problem of complex structure into an engineering problem of controlled state.

02 / Technology platform

Medium Engineering becomes physical through CeStrum.

CeStrum is the technology platform through which the Medium Engineering approach is implemented in physical systems.

Its development is focused on controlled physical effects in complex dispersed media, including short-front shockwave phenomena, cavitation-related effects and changes at interfaces between phases.

Technology platform

CeStrum

A family of engineering solutions for influencing the structural state of complex media and creating conditions for separation, accessibility and resource recovery.

Physical system

CeStrum Reactor

A controlled physical reactor architecture designed to introduce defined energy impulses into a flowing medium.

Proprietary reactor geometry, operating regimes, experimental parameters and other know-how are intentionally not disclosed on this public resource.
