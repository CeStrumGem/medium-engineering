01 / The discipline

Change the medium, not only the process.

Industrial media are rarely simple mixtures. Their behavior can depend on dispersion, aggregation, interfaces, surface effects and structural states.

Medium Engineering starts from a different question: can the state of the medium itself be engineered?

01

Complex medium

Water, minerals, hydrocarbons, residues and dispersed systems.

02

Structural state

Understand interactions, dispersion and inaccessible components.

03

Controlled action

Introduce a defined physical impulse into the system.

04

Accessibility

Enable separation, recovery or treatment.

The central idea

We do not simply treat waste as waste. We engineer the medium until its components become accessible.

02 / Technology platform

Medium Engineering becomes physical through CeStrum.

CeStrum is the technology platform through which the Medium Engineering approach is implemented in physical systems.

Its development is focused on controlled physical effects in complex dispersed media, including short-front shockwave phenomena, cavitation-related effects and changes at interfaces between phases.
