Engineering the medium

We engineer the state of complex media.

Medium Engineering develops physical and process systems for working with complex dispersed media — from contaminated water and mine drainage to hydrocarbons, mineral suspensions and industrial residues.

Explore the technology Start a conversation
01 / Technology

Change the system, not just the chemistry.

Many industrial and environmental problems are not simply chemical. They are problems of structure, interfaces, dispersion, aggregation and accessibility.

Medium Engineering approaches the medium itself as an engineered system. Controlled physical action can destabilize complex structures, accelerate separation and make previously inaccessible components available for recovery or treatment.

02 / Applications

One engineering principle.
Many industrial environments.

01

Mine water & AMD

Treatment and transformation of acidic mine drainage, with a focus on reduced reagent demand, phase separation and recovery of valuable mineral components.

02

Mineral processing

Engineering dispersed mineral systems to improve accessibility, classification, concentration and downstream recovery.

03

Hydrocarbons

Structural treatment of complex hydrocarbon systems, including heavy oil and difficult emulsions, with the objective of improving resource accessibility and process efficiency.

04

Marine & industrial water

New approaches to bilge water, port discharges, industrial residues and other complex aqueous systems where conventional separation becomes expensive or inefficient.

“The objective is not to force a complex medium through a conventional process. It is to understand its structure — and engineer the moment when that structure becomes accessible.”
